    Overview

    This article walks requesters through resetting their ResolveAI account password when they are locked out or the current password has expired. Follow the steps in order — most requesters are back in within two minutes.

    Steps to Reset Your Password

    1. Go to the login page and select Forgot password? underneath the sign-in form.
    2. Enter the email address associated with your account and submit the form.
    3. Check your inbox for an email from no-reply@resolveai.internal. The reset link expires after 30 minutes.
    4. Click the link and choose a new password that meets the complexity requirements (minimum 8 characters, one number, one symbol).
    5. Sign in with your new password. If two-factor authentication is enabled, you will be prompted for your verification code.

    If You Don't Receive the Reset Email

    • Check your spam or junk folder first.
    • Confirm the email address on file is correct by contacting your department administrator.
    • Wait five minutes and use the Resend link option before submitting a support ticket.

    If the issue persists after these steps, submit a ticket under the Password Reset category so an agent can verify your identity and reset the account manually.
